# Go-Live: the story

An original story for Go-Live: Asoka City. The data is `force-app/main/default/staticresources/AsokaCity/story.js`; the vocabulary it uses is [missions-contract.md](missions-contract.md). The player's name and pronouns come from the character creator.

## Act 1: Onboarding

Rhea Okonkwo hires you, a freelance fixer, weeks before Asoka goes live as the city's operating system. Brittle Systems, the incumbent that has run Asoka City since dial-up, is sabotaging the launch through its street muscle, the Legacy Crew. Odd jobs for Rhea (a stolen drive, a router convoy, a compliance officer to deliver) and for Dex Marlow's garage (a stolen car, a race to win back his nephew, a night siege) build to the go/no-go rehearsal on Console Plaza, where Harlan Brittle introduces himself on the phone.

## Act 2: Sprint

With Asoka's launch public, the fight spreads south and east. Lupe Santana's club in the Cadence Quarter is being squeezed for 'legacy support fees'; Patch, a teenage hacker at Kickflip Skate Park, starts picking Brittle's locks; Rhea needs investors driven, relays fixed and her own car escorted to the isles. The act closes on Demo Day: you hold the plaza while Asoka turns every light green, and Harlan, never where the problem is, hints at the docks.

## Act 3: Pipeline

Moss Gantry shows you how Brittle bills the city for cloned servers through the Legacy Crew's yard at the Pipeline. Inspector Ines Vale, the Audit's last straight officer, tests you, arms you with the Kill Switch and builds a case: trucks stalled, a witness escorted, a crooked sergeant photographed, the yard raided and its ledger taken. Meanwhile Dex asks for one small favour too many. The ledger names him; he sells you to Harlan, and the act ends in an ambush and a citywide hunt.

## Act 4: Code Freeze

Wanted by the crooked half of the Audit, you go to ground at the Lazy Loader Motel with Patch and Ivy Strand, Brittle's best engineer turned defector. Code freeze: you recover Ivy's laptop of backdoors, help a suspended Vale steal back her evidence, blow up Brittle's hunter teams with Sticky Notes, copy the city's backups out of Coldline Data Centre, rescue Gantry and Patch, and arrest the commissioner who signed Vale's suspension. Retaking Vale's precinct, you run down Dex, who confesses Harlan's go-live night plan.

## Act 5: Go-Live

Back at the tower, Rhea arms go-live. Lupe turns the Standup into a war room, Ivy plants a surprise in Brittle's outage script, Vale gets a warrant, and you set up failover relays and restart Coldline for the cutover. The assault on Brittle HQ on Preview Point sends Harlan running; he triggers a level-5 Audit override, then makes his last stand at the top of the Go-Live Tower site, where you press the switch and Asoka goes live.
